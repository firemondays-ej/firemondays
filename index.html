    How the 4% rule works
    In 1994, financial researcher William Bengen studied decades of market data and found that withdrawing no more than 4% of your investment portfolio per year means your money lasts at least 30 years — through recessions and market crashes. Your FI number = annual spending ÷ 4% = annual spending × 25. Cross that line and work becomes optional.
